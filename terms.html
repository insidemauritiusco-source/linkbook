LinkBook — Community Rules

Public beta — 8 November 2026. These community guidelines are a beta policy and not a substitute for a reviewed set of legal terms.

Be respectful

  • Do not harass, threaten, bully or target other users.
  • Do not publish hate speech, child abuse material, non-consensual intimate content or material that incites violence.
  • Do not share other people's private personal information without permission.
  • Do not impersonate another person or conduct scams and fraud.
  • Do not spam, use automated abusive accounts, or attempt unauthorized access.
  • Only upload content you own or have permission to share, including photos, videos and music.

Use reporting and blocking

Flag inappropriate posts using the report control. Block members from the Safety & privacy page. Moderators may review reports and remove violating content; review is not guaranteed to be immediate. Contact emergency services for imminent threats.

About Live video

LinkBook Live is experimental and supports a small number of connections, not production-scale broadcasts. Obtain consent before recording or showing others and do not stream content that violates the rules or applicable law.

Account controls

You can export available records and request permanent deletion of your account from Safety & privacy. Deletion is irreversible. You are responsible for the content you publish and for keeping account credentials secure.

Service status

The beta may change or become temporarily unavailable. Platform operator details, applicable legal jurisdiction and full terms should be confirmed before general release.
